Notes
Sofia replaces bmad-spec, bmad-ticket and bmad-correct-course

Requirements, slicing, refining, the board and replanning all go through the PO agent, /bmad-agent-po. Say what you want in plain words; she has no menu to learn. Coming from an older install? Run /bmad setup: it offers to delete the retired skills.

Small change? Skip straight to the build

A small fix needs no planning at all: /bmad-build fix the date format on the invoice page.

Build a whole epic unattended

Once the important decisions are stable, /bmad-build-auto runs one story after another without asking. Review each story, then mark it done with Sofia.

Not sure where you are?

Ask /bmad what's next? It looks at your project and recommends the smallest path that safely fits the work.
